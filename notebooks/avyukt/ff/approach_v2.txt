2. INBOUND DATA GOVERNANCE & LEAKAGE PREVENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prior to modeling, the feature matrices undergo rigorous validation to protect 
against chronological leaks, row-inflation, and dimension mismatches:

* Deterministic Partitioning: Because historical lag processing merges training 
  and testing timelines, the datasets are strictly separated using an explicit 
  binary `is_test` flag during preprocessing. This prevents Day 49 evaluation 
  data from bleeding into the Day 48 training scope.
  
* True Index Extraction: To ensure exact 1:1 mapping with the grading platform, 
  the explicit `Index` column is safely decoupled from the testing feature matrix 
  before tree evaluation to avoid shape contamination.
  
* Symmetrical Feature Assertion: Executes a strict programmatic verification 
  scan that asserts training and testing spaces have perfectly identical columns, 
  eliminating runtime schema crashes.


3. ROBUST VALIDATION STRATEGY: 5-FOLD CROSS-VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To calculate a reliable local evaluation score that scales smoothly to the hidden 
leaderboard, the pipeline implements a rigorous 5-Fold Cross-Validation routine.

* Out-of-Fold (OOF) Splitting: The training space is partitioned into 5 non-
  overlapping segments. Models are trained iteratively on 80% of the 
  observations and generate predictions for the remaining 20%.
