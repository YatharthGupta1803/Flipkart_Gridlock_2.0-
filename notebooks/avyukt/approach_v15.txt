2. PHYSICAL VS. MATHEMATICAL SPATIALITY (THE GRAPH CORRECTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version 15 strictly enforces the law that physical spatial rules (like traffic 
spillover) cannot be calculated on mathematical error terms (residuals). 

* 8-Node Raw Spatial Spillover: Utilizing the `pygeohash` bounding box architecture, 
  the pipeline identifies the exact 8 physical neighbors for every row. It averages 
  the T-24 hour RAW physical traffic of this surrounding network. If Intersection 
  A and B are both gridlocked, the graph explicitly maps the fluid wave of cars 
  approaching the target coordinate.
* Temporal Momentum (Demand Derivative): By calculating the exact delta between 
  T-48 and T-24, the model learns the velocity of traffic growth, shifting from 
  static historical lookup to dynamic vector prediction.


3. ENVIRONMENTAL RISK SCALING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard feature engineering feeds Weather into a model as a static categorical 
variable. However, extreme weather exhibits an exponential relationship with 
underlying traffic capacity—rain on an empty road is irrelevant; rain on a 
highway at rush hour causes immediate gridlock.

* The Interaction Multiplier: The pipeline extracts the Out-of-Fold historical 
  baseline (`TE_geo_time`) and mathematically multiplies it by the localized 
  Weather index. This structural interaction explicitly forces the tree to scale 
  its weather response proportionally to the node's underlying historical congestion.


4. THE DYNAMIC ASYMMETRIC TRIAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To maximize global R² while aggressively capturing micro-anomalies, the base 
engines are structurally diversified at the objective-function level:

1. LightGBM (Dynamic Asymmetric Hunter): The native RMSE objective is overridden 
   with a Custom Dynamic Asymmetric MSE. The penalty multiplier for under-predicting 
   traffic scales continuously with the actual severity of the demand 
   (Multiplier = 1.0 + [y_true * 0.8]). If the model misses a 0.95 gridlock, 
   it faces a severe 1.76x gradient penalty. If it misses a 0.10 quiet road, 
   the penalty drops to nearly 1x, preventing mathematical over-correction.
2. XGBoost (Depth-wise Stabilizer): Maintained on standard symmetric RMSE. Its 
   level-wise growth acts as a conservative anchor against the aggressively penalized 
   LightGBM engine.
3. CatBoost (Categorical Symmetrical): Employs symmetric oblivious trees to process 
   the residual categorical arrays, ensuring deep structural generalization.


5. DRIFT-RESISTANT CONVERGENCE & PLATFORM ASSEMBLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To prevent the temporal Concept Drift inherent in Level-2 Meta-Modeling, the 
pipeline evaluates the Out-of-Fold prediction arrays using a SciPy Nelder-Mead 
optimization simplex. 

* Static Generalization: Instead of learning complex, overfitted temporal rules, 
  the solver explicitly traverses the 3D weight space to find the single most 
  robust, generalized blend for the exact platform grading equation: 
  max(0, 100 * r2_score).
* Physical Bounds Enforcement: The final inference array applies an absolute 
  continuous clip [0.0, 1.0] before being written to disk, guaranteeing flawless 
  evaluation schema compliance.
