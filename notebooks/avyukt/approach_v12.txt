2. AUTOREGRESSIVE MEMORY & SPATIAL TOPOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To maximize baseline accuracy before meta-stacking, the foundational matrix 
incorporates short-term historical context and macroscopic spatial geometry:

* The Autoregressive Injector: The matrix executes exact T-24 and T-48 hour 
  lookbacks. This provides the models with short-term network memory, allowing 
  them to recognize recent road closures or dynamic behavioral shifts that 
  long-term averages miss.
* The Golden Feature (Geo-Time Interaction): The 6-character geohash and the 
  15-minute time slot are concatenated (e.g., `qp02z1_45`). This captures the 
  exact historical rush-hour profile of specific microscopic locations.
* 25-Hub Macroscopic Clustering: Geohashes are decoded into geometric floating 
  points (Lat/Lon) and processed through a K-Means clustering algorithm (k=25). 
  This forces isolated, unmapped nodes into generalized urban neighborhoods.


3. LEAK-FREE TARGET ENCODING (HIGH-CARDINALITY GOVERNANCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The creation of the `geo_time_interaction` key introduces tens of thousands of 
unique categorical variables. 

* Out-of-Fold (OOF) Spatial Mapping: To convert this high-cardinality string into 
  a usable constraint without overfitting, the pipeline employs a strict 5-Fold 
  Target Encoder. The historical demand for any given `geo_time_interaction` is 
  calculated exclusively using validation folds the model was NOT trained on, 
  mathematically preventing the trees from memorizing the training target.


4. LEVEL-1 ALGORITHMIC TRIAD (BASE ENGINES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Level-1 architecture deploys three structurally distinct gradient-boosted 
topologies. This structural diversity is strictly required for Level-2 error 
cancellation to function effectively:

1. LightGBM (Leaf-wise Growth): Configured with `num_leaves=128` and `max_depth=8`. 
   The highly efficient leaf-wise splitting acts as a "sniper," capturing deep, 
   isolated feature interactions and extreme traffic spikes.
2. XGBoost (Depth-wise Growth): Utilizes a level-wise growth strategy capped at 
   `max_depth=7`. This provides a structurally conservative baseline, preventing 
   localized overfitting.
3. CatBoost (Symmetric Trees): Employs symmetric oblivious trees where the same 
   splitting criteria is applied across an entire layer. This acts as a robust 
   mathematical regularizer against spatial noise.


5. LEVEL-2 META-MODEL STACKING (THE 93.0 PUSH)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Instead of relying on a static, global optimization heuristic (like Nelder-Mead) 
to blend the models, V12 treats the Level-1 predictions as a brand new dataset:

* Ridge Regression (Meta-Model): The Level-1 OOF predictions are stacked 
  horizontally and fed into a dynamically cross-validated Ridge Regressor (RidgeCV).
* Dynamic Trust Allocation: The Ridge model analyzes millions of Level-1 prediction 
  errors and learns localized trust coefficients. For example, it autonomously 
  learns to aggressively weight LightGBM during rush hours but shifts trust to 
  CatBoost for low-variance night traffic.
* Physical Bounds Enforcement: Traffic capacity cannot drop below zero or exceed 
  100%. The final Level-2 inference array applies an absolute continuous clip 
  [0.0, 1.0] before being bound to the test index and written to disk.
