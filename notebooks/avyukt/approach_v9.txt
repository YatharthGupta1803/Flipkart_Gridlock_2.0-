2. CHRONOLOGICAL INTEGRITY: WALK-FORWARD VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard randomized K-Fold validation induces severe chronological leakage in 
traffic forecasting, allowing models to learn from future days to predict the 
past. V9 completely overhauls the validation matrix:

* Time-Series Splitting (Walk-Forward): The matrix is sorted strictly by day 
  and timestamp. The validation engine only trains on Day 1 through N, and 
  validates exclusively on Day N+1. This physically simulates the exact hidden 
  evaluation constraints of the production server.
* The Weekly Cycle Lag (168-Hour): Traffic is driven by a 7-day cycle. In 
  addition to standard 24H and 48H lags, the pipeline explicitly engineers a 
  Day-7 (168-Hour) memory array, locking weekend and weekday baselines directly 
  into the feature space.


3. TWEEDIE OBJECTIVE & TARGET KINEMATICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error) functions apply symmetrical penalties, 
forcing decision trees to over-smooth predictions and miss extreme traffic jams 
in an attempt to avoid variance penalties on empty roads.

* Zero-Inflated Physics Integration: V9 shifts both LightGBM and XGBoost to a 
  Tweedie Regression objective (variance_power=1.5). This mathematical distribution 
  is explicitly engineered for datasets featuring a massive cluster of exact 
  zeros (empty night roads) paired with a long right-tail of extreme positive 
  values (gridlock spikes).


4. SPATIAL HIERARCHY & GHOST GEOHASH ISOLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Treating spatial coordinates as isolated integers prevents the trees from 
understanding neighborhood bleed and adjacent traffic flow.

* Macroscopic Neighborhood Flow: The pipeline programmatically extracts the 
  5-character Parent Geohash from the standard 6-character coordinate. By 
  calculating the mean demand of the parent zone, the model simulates spatial 
  adjacency without the computational overhead of KDTree geographic mapping.
* Ghost Node Absolute Zero Imputation: Standard pipelines impute missing history 
  for unseen test-set geohashes using the global city average, injecting false 
  traffic. V9 isolates these missing nodes and forces a strict 0.0 imputation, 
  mathematically instructing the model that unmapped zones hold zero capacity.


5. ALGORITHMIC MULTI-ENGINE CORE & TEMPORAL DECAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The ensemble deploys two elite gradient-boosted frameworks, dynamically 
weighted during training to account for concept drift over the 60-day horizon:

* Exponential Temporal Decay: Cities are physically dynamic; road closures and 
  behavior shift over time. Training rows are subjected to a decay function: 
  exp((day - max_day) / 15.0). This forces the gradient descent to prioritize 
  recent topographical traffic states and slowly "forget" stale, 60-day-old data.
* LightGBM (Leaf-wise Tweedie): Configured with constrained tree depth (max=8) 
  to execute highly efficient, regularized cuts across the zero-inflated target.
* XGBoost (Depth-wise Tweedie): Utilizes level-wise growth (max=7) to provide 
  a mathematically resilient, structurally conservative baseline.

* Physical Bounds Enforcement: Traffic cannot drop below 0% capacity. The final
