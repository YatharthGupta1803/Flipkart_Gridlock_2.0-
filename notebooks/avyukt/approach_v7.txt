2. SPATIOTEMPORAL FEATURE ENGINEERING & DATA GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The dataset requires moving beyond standard tabular processing to capture real-
world traffic physics. We engineered a robust 38-dimension feature matrix:

* Deep Historical Memory (48H Lags): Traffic obeys strict multi-day cycles. 
  We engineered a rolling lag mechanism by mathematically shifting the training 
  time matrix forward (Day+1 and Day+2). This maps exact historical demand 
  directly to the current query while inherently avoiding chronological leakage.

* Macro-Hub K-Means Clustering: Standard geohashes are rigid constraints. To 
  map organic city traffic, we deployed K-Means clustering across the spatial 
  coordinate space. We strictly constrained this to 25 Macro-Hubs to force the 
  algorithms to learn broad, highly generalizable traffic corridors rather than 
  overfitting to microscopic neighborhood intersections.

* Trigonometric Temporal Constraints: Tree algorithms misinterpret 23:59 and 
  00:01 as numerical opposites. We applied sine and cosine transformations to 
  the hour and minute vectors, mapping time to a continuous geometric circle.


3. TARGET COMPRESSION & LEAKAGE-FREE PROFILING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Traffic demand exhibits an inherently skewed distribution, ranging from near-zero 
lulls to massive, unpredictable capacity spikes.

* Logarithmic Target Transformation: To prevent gradient boosting models from 
  heavily penalizing themselves on absolute error variance during these spikes, 
  the raw demand array is compressed into log-space via y_log = ln(1 + y_raw) 
  prior to training. The models train strictly on this logarithmically bounded 
  target, resulting in a significantly smoother error surface.

* K-Fold Target Encoding: To map the historical priority of individual spatial 
  nodes, we engineered an Out-of-Fold (OOF) target encoding loop for specific 
  geohashes. By processing the training matrix through an isolated 5-Fold split, 
  the historical mean demand was calculated EXCLUSIVELY using records the model 
  was not currently evaluating, guaranteeing zero look-ahead bias.


4. MULTI-ENGINE ENSEMBLE CONFIGURATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Our predictive engine leverages a triplet of diverse gradient boosting frameworks, 
all heavily regularized to prioritize generalization over memorization:

* LightGBM (Leaf-Wise Growth): Constrained tree depth (max_depth=8) and high 
  child-sample minimums (min_child_samples=20) to penalize over-segmentation.
* XGBoost (Depth-Wise Regularization): Configured with max_depth=7 to build 
  structurally conservative, mathematically resilient decision trees.
* CatBoost (Symmetric Topology): Configured with depth=8, excelling natively 
  at processing our highly-cardinal temporal and spatial categorical crosses.


5. META-OPTIMIZATION: NELDER-MEAD SIMPLEX BLENDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rather than estimating arbitrary ensemble weights, the pipeline mathematically 
aligns directly with the leaderboard metric.

The algorithm's OOF arrays are first inverted from log-space back to raw physical 
space. These arrays are then passed into a SciPy Nelder-Mead simplex solver. 
The solver's objective function is mathematically hardcoded to maximize the 
platform's exact metric: max(0, 100 * R2). This forces the solver to output 
the mathematically absolute optimal blending ratios for the competition.


6. POST-PROCESSING, TARGET INVERSION & OPERATIONAL EXPORT LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before the final prediction arrays are streamed to disk, they pass through a 
rigorous mathematical inversion and boundary constraint filter:

* Exponential Target Inversion: Since the target variable profile was compressed 
  during preprocessing using a natural log transformation, the blended ensemble 
  predictions (y_log) are converted back to raw traffic proportions using the 
  natural exponential inverse: y_raw = exp(y_log) - 1.

* Physical Target Bound Enforcements: Real-world traffic capacity cannot drop 
  below 0% or exceed 100%. The pipeline applies a strict non-negative clipping 
  threshold [0.0 to 1.0] to protect against mathematically impossible fractional 
  anomalies.

* Platform Schema Formatting: The final data structure is dynamically mapped 
  to output exactly 41,778 rows mapping perfectly to the required case-sensitive 
  system headers: "Index" and "demand". Standard Pandas indexing is omitted via 
  index=False to ensure instant grading compatibility with the evaluation server.
