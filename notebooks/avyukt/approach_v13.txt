2. SPATIAL GRAPH ADJACENCY & TRAJECTORY KINEMATICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard machine learning models evaluate geohashes as isolated islands. Version 13 
recognizes that traffic obeys the laws of fluid dynamics—gridlocks spill over 
into adjacent physical spaces.

* 8-Node Spatial Spillover: Utilizing the `pygeohash` bounding box architecture, 
  the pipeline identifies the exact 8 physical neighbors (North, South, East, West, 
  and diagonals) for every row. It averages the T-24 hour traffic of this surrounding 
  network, allowing the model to "see" traffic waves approaching a target intersection.
* Temporal Momentum (Demand Derivative): Static lags (24H/48H) provide memory, but 
  momentum provides trajectory. By calculating the exact delta between T-48 and 
  T-24, the model learns the velocity of traffic growth, shifting from static 
  forecasting to dynamic vector prediction.
* The Golden Feature (Geo-Time Interaction): The precise rush-hour profile of 
  specific microscopic locations is maintained by concatenating the geohash and 
  15-minute time slot (e.g., `qp02z1_45`).


3. LEAK-FREE TARGET ENCODING (HIGH-CARDINALITY GOVERNANCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To leverage the massive categorical complexity of the `geo_time_interaction` key, 
the architecture continues to rely on rigorous K-Fold mapping.

* Out-of-Fold (OOF) Spatial Mapping: The historical demand for any given spatial 
  interaction is calculated exclusively using cross-validation folds the model was 
  NOT trained on. This converts a hyper-sparse string into a mathematically 
  continuous constraint with absolutely zero look-ahead data leakage.


4. THE ASYMMETRIC ALGORITHMIC TRIAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To cross the 92.0 threshold, the base engines must stop treating all errors equally. 
Missing a massive traffic jam is strategically worse than misjudging an empty road.

1. LightGBM (The Asymmetric Hunter): The native RMSE objective is completely 
   overridden with a Custom Asymmetric Mean Squared Error gradient. By applying 
   a 1.5x mathematical penalty multiplier to under-predictions, the leaf-wise 
   growth algorithm is forced to aggressively hunt for hidden traffic spikes.
2. XGBoost (Depth-wise Stabilizer): Maintained on standard RMSE. Its level-wise 
   growth strategy acts as a conservative anchor, preventing the aggressive 
   LightGBM engine from hallucinating traffic jams where none exist.
3. CatBoost (Categorical Symmetrical): Employs symmetric oblivious trees to 
   regularize the spatial noise, processing the residual categorical arrays 
   seamlessly.


5. DRIFT-RESISTANT CONVERGENCE & PLATFORM ASSEMBLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version 12 (Level-2 Stacking) collapsed due to Temporal Concept Drift—the 
Meta-Model over-optimized on the past and failed on the future. Version 13 corrects 
this by reverting to generalized mathematical convergence.

* Nelder-Mead Optimization: Independent Out-of-Fold (OOF) prediction arrays from 
  the Triad are passed into a SciPy Nelder-Mead Simplex. Instead of learning 
  complex, overfitted temporal rules, the solver explicitly traverses the weight 
  space to find the single most robust, generalized blend for the exact grading 
  equation: max(0, 100 * r2_score).
* Physical Bounds Enforcement: The final inference array applies an absolute 
  continuous clip [0.0, 1.0] before being bound to the test index and written 
  to disk, guaranteeing flawless evaluation schema compliance.
