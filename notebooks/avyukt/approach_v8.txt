2. SPATIAL COLD-START: K-NEAREST NEIGHBORS (KNN) FALLBACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Day 49 evaluation timeline contains unmapped geohash sectors with zero 
historical representation. To prevent the degradation of local spatial accuracy 
caused by global-mean imputation, the pipeline implements a dynamic spatial fallback:

* Base-32 Decoding: Alphanumeric grid codes are decoded into geometric latitude 
  and longitude floating-point coordinates.
* Surgical KNN Imputation: A strict spatial K-Nearest Neighbors regressor (k=3) 
  is fitted solely on the historical spatial distribution. For unseen test 
  intersections, the model mathematically interpolates the expected baseline 
  traffic demand based on the actual behavior of the three closest physical neighbors.


3. LEAK-FREE TARGET ENCODING & KINEMATICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Urban traffic flow relies on spatial baselines and cyclical time bounds. 

* Out-of-Fold (OOF) Spatial Mapping: Rather than merging static global averages 
  which causes severe data leakage, the pipeline engineers an OOF Target Encoder. 
  The models receive localized historical traffic constraints derived exclusively 
  from cross-validation folds they were not trained on.
* Cyclical Projections: Discrete timestamps are projected onto a continuous 2D 
  plane using sine and cosine waves, allowing tree models to recognize 11:45 PM 
  and 00:00 AM as adjacent temporal bounds.


4. ALGORITHMIC MULTI-ENGINE CORE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The ensemble leverages model diversity by deploying three distinct gradient-
boosted decision tree backends, maximizing localized interaction mapping:

1. LightGBM (Leaf-wise Growth): Configured with an aggressive num_leaves parameter 
   (256), highly efficient leaf-wise splitting captures deep, high-order feature 
   interactions across complex spatio-temporal grids.
2. XGBoost (Depth-wise Growth): Utilizes a level-wise growth strategy that 
   regularizes tree depth uniformly, providing a highly stable structural 
   baseline that prevents localized overfitting on high-variance data points.
3. CatBoost (Symmetric Trees): Employs symmetric oblivious trees where the 
   same splitting criteria is applied across an entire layer, acting as a robust 
   regularizer against localized spatial noise.


5. DIRECT METRIC OPTIMIZATION & PLATFORM ASSEMBLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Instead of relying on loss-function proxies, the pipeline predicts on the direct 
physical scale and optimizes identically to the platform backend:

* R² Maximization Solver: Independent Out-of-Fold (OOF) prediction arrays from 
  the validation sets are passed into a Nelder-Mead Simplex. The solver 
  explicitly optimizes the weighting coefficients (w1, w2, w3) to maximize the 
  exact grading metric: max(0, 100 * r2_score).
* Dynamic Schema Enforcement: A programmatic enforcer scans and strips mismatched 
  columns before inference, guaranteeing 100% matrix alignment.
* Physical Bounds Enforcement: Traffic cannot theoretically drop below zero. 
  The final array applies an absolute non-negative clip [0.0, 1.0] before being 
  bound to the exact 41,778 row evaluation constraints for submission.
